A substantial family home on one of Salthill's finest roads.

Niall Browne & Co., are delighted to present Loudres Villa, a substantial five-bedroom semi-detached home on Dr

Mannix Road, one of Salthill's most established and sought-after residential addresses. Extending to approximately 161 sq m (1,733 sq ft), this owner-occupied home has been well maintained and combines generous family accommodation with a one-bedroom self-contained unit.

Quality finishes are evident from the moment you step inside, with V'Soske Joyce carpets to the hall and landing and fine marble fireplaces in both reception rooms. The first floor offers four double bedrooms, two of which have fitted wardrobes, together with a fully tiled family bathroom. A fully insulated attic, accessed by stairs, provides valuable additional storage.

The self-contained unit, with its own front door, comprises a double bedroom, kitchen and bathroom — ideal for extended family, visiting guests or a home office. Planning permission is in place for the extension. Outside, the property enjoys a large site with a south-facing rear garden and a spacious separate garage, while ample room to the side provides additional car parking and scope for future development, subject to planning permission. All of this is within a short stroll of Salthill Promenade, the village and some of the city's most popular schools.
