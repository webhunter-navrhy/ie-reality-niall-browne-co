Lakeview Hill, Site 14, Barnacranny, Bushypark, Galway

A rare opportunity to acquire a serviced site with full planning permission in one of Bushypark's most sought-after settings. Site 14 extends to c. 0.232ha (0.57 acres) and comes with full planning permission (Ref. 21/161) for a striking contemporary six-bedroom residence of approximately 418 sq.m (4,500 sq.ft) over two floors. Site groundworks are already complete and the approved dwelling has not been built, giving a purchaser the freedom to build exactly to this design or bring their own vision to a site with permission already secured.

Set within an established enclave of individually designed homes, several already built or under construction, Lakeview Hill enjoys an elevated position close to the River Corrib and Lough Corrib, with the whole of Galway city, NUIG and Bushypark village all within easy reach.

At a glance:

Full planning permission granted (Ref. 21/161) for a two-storey, six-bedroom dwelling with roof terrace

Site groundworks already completed — tarmacadam road to frontage, water, foul and storm connections at the boundary

No VAT applicable · sold with no encumbrances 418 sq.m approved floor area across ground and first floor, six bedrooms and seven bathrooms

Natural stone and render facade with feature aluminium flashing

Elevated site with mature boundary planting and existing stone wall retained

Neighbouring sites at Lakeview Hill already granted permission and under construction

A revised-design application (adding a roof terrace with 42 solar panels) is currently under consideration by Galway City Council on foot of the existing permission; full details are available on request.
